Questions

Will it run on my computer?

On a Mac, you need Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 Sonoma or newer. Intel Macs are not supported. To check: Apple menu > About This Mac. Chip should say Apple M1 or later (M2, M3 and so on), and macOS should be 14 or higher.

On Windows, you need Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. To check: Settings > System > About. System type should say 64-bit.

Lastbook doesn’t run on phones or tablets.

How much space do I need?

Choose Small (7.7 GB), Medium (67.5 GB), or All (about 388 GB). Local AI adds about 17 GB. Your selected state map and address index add more, with sizes shown in setup. Mac setup checks free space before downloading.

Does it need the internet?

Internet is needed to download the app, library and optional AI. Once setup finishes, reading, maps, videos and AI work offline.

What is Chat?

Chat uses the optional local Gemma model to answer questions from your library and link to its sources. It is available in the Mac, Windows and Linux apps.

Is the medical information checked?

It comes from published guides, such as Hesperian, the World Health Organization and the Red Cross. Lastbook itself is not clinically reviewed, and it doesn’t replace trained care. In an emergency, call 911 if you can.

Is there a bigger library?

Yes. Small (7.7 GB) and Medium (67.5 GB) are available. All (about 388 GB) is also available. Mac setup shows the available choices.
